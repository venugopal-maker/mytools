Video to text or audio.

Made for long files, even 3 hours. It runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded and nothing needs installing.

Drop a video or audio file here, or click to choose MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG. Any length.

Translation runs inside Chrome on your computer (Chrome 138 or newer). Chrome downloads a small language pack the first time. Malayalam and Urdu are not supported for translation yet.

Keep this tab open and your laptop plugged in while it works. The first run downloads the model once; after that it is cached. Chrome or Edge on a computer with a GPU is the fastest.
